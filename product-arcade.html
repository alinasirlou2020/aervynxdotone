05 / TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & FAIRNESS

Real-Time Gameplay Off-Chain.
Ownership & Rewards Verified.

Putting every frame update or collision on-chain would ruin responsiveness. Aervynx separates high-speed off-chain gameplay execution from blockchain ownership verification and controlled reward settlement.

PROPOSED HYBRID STACK
STEP 01 · ON-CHAIN / AUTH NFT Equipment Wallet ownership & delegated usage check
STEP 02 · OFF-CHAIN RT Game Engine 60FPS physics, combat & module effects
STEP 03 · VALIDATION Verified Score Server-side logs & anomaly detection
STEP 04 · SNAPSHOT Reward Eligibility Campaign rules & period snapshot
STEP 05 · SETTLEMENT Ecosystem Distribution Genesis access, discounts & DOTO pool
01 · NFT Ownership & Equipment Validation (On-Chain Read + Session Sync)

Verifies whether the player owns or holds authorized temporary access to specific equipment NFTs, checks slot compatibility, and synchronizes loadout attributes at session start. Subsequent NFT transfers refresh inventory state at defined session boundaries.

FAIRNESS & ANTI-ABUSE PRINCIPLE: PLAYERS SHOULD BE REWARDED FOR VERIFIED PERFORMANCE, NOT FOR MANIPULATING THE SCORING SYSTEM.
01 · SERVER VALIDATION

Client-side scores are never blindly trusted; session telemetry and mission logs are validated server-side.

02 · SIGNED MATCH RESULTS

Completed sessions produce structured, non-reusable result digests with rate limits and replay protection.

03 · SYBIL & BOT CONTROLS

Behavioral anomaly checks and account-eligibility guards mitigate multi-account farming and automated scripts.

04 · DUPLICATE CLAIM GUARD

Every reward period snapshot and distribution record carries a unique identifier to prevent double-claiming.
