PRINCIPLE 01 · ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY

Digital treasury security requires more than storing assets. It requires controlling who can act, when they can act, and when operations must stop. ZeroVault is not a simple wallet interface—it is a modular smart contract security and execution-control architecture.

PRINCIPLE 02 · SEPARATION OF INTERFACE & CUSTODY

Instead of allowing unrestricted direct treasury operations, ZeroVault establishes multiple layers of protection between treasury assets held in TreasuryVault and the operational calls routed through the Treasury interface.

PRINCIPLE 03 · COORDINATED CONTROL LAYERS

Every sensitive operation is governed by three coordinated control layers: RoleManager enforces role-based permission boundaries, Timelock introduces a mandatory review delay between scheduling and execution, and EmergencyPause enables rapid restriction of protected operations during unexpected incidents.

03 · CONTROLLED EXECUTION LIFECYCLE

Authorization, Delay & Execution

Sensitive treasury operations cannot execute instantaneously. ZeroVault separates proposing an action from executing it through an enforced Timelock waiting window, while maintaining an independent EmergencyPause restriction path.

SENSITIVE OPERATION LIFECYCLE WALKTHROUGH STAGE 1 OF 4 · ROLE AUTHORIZATION CHECK
STEP 01 RoleManager.sol

Authorization Check

Caller role is verified against RoleManager permission boundaries. Unauthorized callers revert immediately via custom errors.

STEP 02 Timelock.sol

Operation Scheduling

The authorized operation is proposed and scheduled on-chain, emitting an event and locking in its earliest execution timestamp.

STEP 03 Timelock.sol

Mandatory Delay Window

The operation enters a mandatory waiting period. Execution attempts before the required delay elapses are rejected.

STEP 04 Treasury → TreasuryVault

Controlled Execution

Once the delay passes and EmergencyPause is inactive, an authorized signer executes the ETH or ERC-20 withdrawal via Treasury.

SEPARATE CONTROL PATH · EMERGENCYPAUSE.SOL Restricts protected treasury and withdrawal operations when active. (Note: Pausing restricts execution flow; it does not reverse completed on-chain transactions.)
04 · IMPLEMENTATION-LEVEL SECURITY

Security Engineering & Asset Discipline

ZeroVault implements established Solidity defensive engineering practices across its access-control, state-transition, and asset-handling layers.

Access & Execution

LAYER 01
  • 01
    Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Dedicated RoleManager restricts privileged treasury operations to explicitly authorized roles.
  • 02
    Timelock-Controlled Operations Sensitive withdrawals and state changes must be scheduled and await a mandatory delay before execution.
  • 03
    Emergency Pause Mechanism Dedicated EmergencyPause component halts protected withdrawal operations during security interventions.

Contract Defenses

LAYER 02
  • 01
    CEI Pattern & Reentrancy Protection Strict Checks-Effects-Interactions ordering combined with reentrancy guards on external value transfers.
  • 02
    Custom Errors & Immutable Variables Gas-efficient custom Solidity errors for explicit revert reasons and immutable references where appropriate.
  • 03
    Zero-Address Validation & Pull-Payment Input sanitization against 0x0 addresses, pull-payment patterns where applicable, and comprehensive event emission.

Asset Management

LAYER 03
  • 01
    Native ETH Handling Supports both direct ETH deposits and Treasury-routed ETH flows into protected TreasuryVault custody.
  • 02
    ERC-20 Token Handling Structured custody and accounting for standard ERC-20 treasury tokens within TreasuryVault.
  • 03
    Controlled Withdrawal Operations Separates the operational Treasury interface from TreasuryVault storage so withdrawals follow the authorized flow.
