02 · FRACTIONAL SPV FLEETS

Tokenized Commercial Vehicles

Unlock the sharing economy's true potential with our Tokenized Commercial Vehicles. We have engineered a legally compliant, trustless framework where physical fleets—ranging from delivery vans to transit vehicles—are encapsulated within dedicated Special Purpose Vehicles (SPV)s.

These legal wrappers are natively tokenized on the DotOne Chain, granting users undisputed fractional ownership and a direct claim to the fleet's daily commercial cash flows. Operating in total synergy with our logistics ecosystem, these vehicles generate constant on-chain revenue through rental and cargo transport operations.

The traditional barriers to heavy asset investment are entirely removed. Now, you can instantly buy, trade, or stake your shares of a physical vehicular fleet, turning urban mobility and logistics into a decentralized, liquid, and relentlessly yield-generating asset class in your Web3 portfolio.
