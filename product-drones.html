01 · THE ECONOMIC PROPOSITION

50/50 Net Distributable Profit Split

AERVYNX Drone RWA (FlightYield) is engineered around a strict, period-based accounting rule: 50% of the fleet's eligible net distributable operating profit is allocated to the Token Holder Distribution Pool, and 50% is allocated to the Fleet Operator.

CONTRACTUAL PROFIT ENTITLEMENT ARCHITECTURE SPLIT APPLIES STRICTLY TO ELIGIBLE NET DISTRIBUTABLE PROFIT — NEVER GROSS REVENUE
