THE TRUST GAP IN GLOBAL LOGISTICS

For decades, global e-commerce has suffered from a fundamental flaw: when customers pay online, they trust the merchant to ship and the carrier to deliver. With Cash-on-Delivery, merchants risk package rejection and heavy return costs, while false chargeback claims cost billions annually. Paper signatures, centralized databases, and QR codes are easily spoofed.

HARDWARE ENCLAVE + EIP-712 OFFLINE SIGNATURE

DeliveryEscrow never relies on hackable GPS coordinates or Web2 APIs. When the buyer taps their AERVYNX.One hardware card against the drone's 13.56MHz NFC module, its internal EAL6+ Secure Enclave generates an offline EIP-712 signature over cargoId, recipient, timestamp, and a one-time nonce.

ERC-4337 GASLESS SETTLEMENT IN 3.0 SECONDS

The drone relays the NFC signature to the DotOne RPC, where DeliveryEscrow.sol uses ECDSA.recover to verify the buyer's public key and executes settleDelivery(). Routed through our ERC-4337 Account Abstraction Paymaster, the buyer pays zero direct gas fees—and with DotOne's 3.0s finality, funds settle before the drone turns around.
