01 · FRACTIONAL SPV FLEETS

The SPV is the Bridge.

Unlock the sharing economy's potential with Tokenized Commercial Vehicles. Physical fleets—from delivery vans to transit vehicles—are designed to be encapsulated within dedicated Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs).

These legal wrappers provide the structural layer between physical assets and their digital representation. The corresponding participation tokens can be issued on DotOne Chain 505 according to the final legal and technical framework.
